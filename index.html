Course Goals


1. Consider the role of rhetoric, technology, and ethics in acts of making.

In the act of creation, utilizing rhetorical, technological, and ethical ideas curate stronger outcomes. When spawning ideas from scratch, ethical ideals play a vital role in determining direction in making. Rhetorical ideas provide a sense of self and personalization in creativity. I aim to master being able to use all of these ideas simoultaneously in my process of creation in order to make the best finished product possible.

2. Leverage Design Principles.

Using design principles in effective ways can help me create more impactful and personal designs. I hope to be able to use these principles affectively and together to make the best possible design.

3. Engage with others in a workshop environment.

Collaborating with peers in the classroom is very important to me, seeing as I am new to the world of design, getting feedback and instruction from my peers has been impactful and I assume it will continue to be. Additionally, I believe that I can provide and fresh and outside perspective on design that my peers who are very well-versed in desgin may not see.



4. Practice revision within and across media forms.

I am to use peer criticism and even instructor narration along with my personal reflections to improve my designs. And as my revisions progress, I hope that a lot of issues I intially do not recognize now, I will be able to in the future.

5. Learn to present your work.

Presenting my work and speaking to a large group of people is something I am familiar with, however, with this class and repetitiveness of presentations will improve my communication skills within the classroom. I am to better address a group by moving within the room and looking at everyone present equally.
